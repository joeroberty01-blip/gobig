# Competitor analysis

First pass 2026-10-10 (desk research; sites visited the same day). We only state what we could see.
"Not stated" means the public page did not say — not that the feature is missing.

## The real competition is informal

| Alternative | What customers get | Weakness we can beat | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Word of mouth / neighbours / estate WhatsApp groups** | Trusted names, free | Slow; limited to who you know; no recourse; no price reference | [A] + regional reports |
| **Instagram & WhatsApp Business pages** | Photos, direct chat | Hard to compare; fake accounts and negative comments are a known problem for SMEs | UDSM SME study (2016) |
| **Jiji.co.tz** (classifieds) | Large listing volume across categories, incl. services and tradespeople's CVs | Not built for services: no verification of skills, no structured requests or quotes, no job history; monetises by paid boosts | [Jiji](https://jiji.co.tz/sw/manual-labour-cvs); model per [ProductMint](https://productmint.com/how-does-jiji-make-money/) |
| **Bolt** (rides, deliveries) | 8 cities, 30,000+ drivers; Uber left Jan 2026 | We should **not** compete on rides | [The Chanzo, Feb 2026](https://thechanzo.com/2026/02/01/uber-exits-tanzania-amidst-regulatory-squeeze-and-fierce-competition/) |

## What this tells us

1. **No one has won Dar yet** [R]: the visible platforms are small, multi-country or unclear. The
   market is still word of mouth + Instagram/WhatsApp + Jiji.
2. **Supply depth in a few places beats breadth everywhere.** Gig-fully's "0 gigs open now" and
   small counts show the trap: many categories and cities, thin liquidity in each.
3. **Trust + speed is the gap**: nobody visible offers *proven* response times, a real job history
   tied to completed requests, and a simple guarantee. Go Big already has the plumbing for the first
   two (response-time badges, verified-job reviews) — but no businesses using it yet.
4. **Monetisation that worked elsewhere charges on success** (Kandua) and bundles business tools,
   rather than charging to be listed.
