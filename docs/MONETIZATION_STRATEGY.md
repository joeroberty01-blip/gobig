# Monetisation strategy

2026-10-10. No prices in this document are decided — price points are **experiments for the owner
to approve**. Built today: Free / Pro / Professional / Featured plans (unpriced), sponsored
campaigns (always labelled), manual payment recording, admin free trials. Nothing charges automatically.

## Principle

Businesses pay **after** Go Big has visibly sent them customers. Charging to be listed fails in a
market where Instagram, WhatsApp and Jiji listings are free [R]. Trust is the product: paid placement
stays labelled and outside the trust ranking and verification, always.

## Options assessed

| Model | Willingness to pay | Cost to run | Risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pay to be listed | Low — free alternatives everywhere [A] | Low | Empties supply | **No** |
| **Pro subscription** (monthly: more leads per day, priority on requests, business tools, analytics, extra photos) | Medium **once leads arrive** [A] | Low (SMS + support) | Churn if leads dry up | **Yes, stage 1** |
| **Sponsored placement** (labelled, capped slots, targeted by service × area) | Medium for established businesses [A] | Low | Must never look organic | **Yes, stage 2** |
| **Pay per qualified lead** (credits bought with mobile money) | Medium; clear value per lead [A] | Needs payment collection | "Lead was bad" disputes | **Test in stage 2** |
| **Fee per job won** (Kandua model [R]) | Higher acceptance (pay only on success) | Needs in-app payment or trust-based invoicing | Off-platform leakage | **Stage 3**, after payments exist |
| **B2B accounts** (property managers, offices, schools: multi-site requests, monthly invoice) | Higher: businesses already pay for maintenance [A] | Account management | Sales effort | **Pilot in stage 2** |
| Commission on bookings | Requires escrow + payments licence partner | High | Leakage, regulation | **Later** |
| Display advertising | Low until traffic exists | Low | Clutters a trust product | **No (for now)** |
| Insurance/warranty partner | Partner pays for distribution [R: Kandua/Santam] | Partner ops | Claims handling | **Explore in P3** |

## Recommended path

**Stage 0 — prove liquidity (months 0–3): everything free.** Goal: in 3 services × 3–5 neighbourhoods,
≥70% of requests get a reply within 30 minutes and ≥40% end in a hire. Revenue: none. Cost: hosting +
SMS + field onboarding.

**Stage 1 — Pro plan pilot (month 3–4).** Offer Pro only to businesses that received ≥5 leads in the
free tier. Test 2–3 price points (owner to set; e.g. a low, middle and higher monthly figure in TZS)
with 10–20 businesses each, paid by M-Pesa/Mixx/Airtel to a till and recorded by admin (already built).
Free plan keeps a fair daily lead cap so the marketplace stays useful.

**Stage 2 — sponsored slots + B2B pilot + lead credits test (months 5–9).** Capped, labelled sponsored
slots per service × area; 2–3 property-manager accounts; a lead-credit test in one category.

**Stage 3 — payments & success fees (9+ months).** Integrate a BoT-licensed aggregator (Selcom /
AzamPay / ClickPesa — fees not published, ask for quotes [R]); only then a fee per job won / escrow.

## Unit economics (rough, to refine)

| Item | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| SMS per request (3–5 businesses alerted + 2 customer updates) | TZS ~100–150 | ~TZS 20/SMS local vendor [R] |
| WhatsApp utility message (if used) | ~US$0.02–0.03 each | vendor estimate [R]; Meta rate card to confirm |
| Go Big AI per question | ~US$0.01 with ~2k input / 200 output tokens on Claude Opus 5.5 ($4 / $20 per M tokens) | [A] estimate; capped by the AI cost guard |
| Hosting | paid Render plan + Neon | check current Render/Neon pricing [A] |
| Field onboarding | the biggest cost: agent time per live business | [A] — measure in the concierge test |

A Pro subscriber pays for itself if they get a few jobs a month they would not otherwise have had —
the weekly proof-of-value SMS must make that visible.

## Guardrails (unchanged)

No invented prices; no hidden paid ranking; verification can't be bought; no automatic charges;
every price change approved by the owner and audited.
